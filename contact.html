Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our services and processes

What areas in South Africa do you serve? +
We serve clients across South Africa, with a strong presence in major agricultural regions. Our services are available nationwide, and we can arrange consultations and site visits regardless of location.
How quickly can you start a new project? +
After initial consultation and agreement, we can typically begin small to medium projects within 1-2 weeks. Larger commercial projects may require 3-4 weeks for proper planning and resource allocation.
Do you offer emergency services for livestock? +
Yes, we provide 24/7 emergency services for critical livestock situations. Our team is available for urgent consultations and on-site assistance for animal health emergencies.
What makes your poultry services different? +
Our poultry services combine traditional expertise with modern technology, focusing on sustainable practices, animal welfare, and optimal production efficiency. We provide comprehensive solutions from breeding to market-ready products.
Can you help with agricultural funding applications? +
While we don't directly provide funding, we assist clients with business plans, project proposals, and documentation needed for agricultural funding applications through various government and private sector programs.
