Who We Are

Leading Agricultural Excellence

A proudly South African enterprise dedicated to sustainable farming and quality production

Shauny and Donny Tradings and Projects (Pty) Ltd is a proudly South African agricultural enterprise dedicated to the sustainable production of poultry, livestock, eggs, and crops. We are committed to innovation, quality, and ethical farming practices that promote community nourishment and contribute to a thriving agricultural sector.

Our operations combine traditional farming expertise with modern technologies to ensure consistent quality, high yield, and environmental responsibility.
